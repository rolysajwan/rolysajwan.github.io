Building a spiritual heritage brand from the ground up.

DevGanga Amrit is a premium spiritual heritage brand inspired by the sacred significance of the Ganga and Devprayag, Uttarakhand. The project aims to connect this heritage with considered packaging, clear product information and a thoughtful digital experience.

Intended useReligious rituals only

More than a product idea. A brand taking shape.

The work moves from positioning and visual identity to early digital and operational foundations. Concepts and prototypes stay clearly separate from finished, production-ready assets.

Vision & positioning

  • Heritage: respect for the Ganga and Devprayag
  • Presentation: elegant identity and packaging
  • Accessibility: clear digital touchpoints

Product concept

  • Devprayag Ganga Jal, 250 ml bottle
  • Daily puja, abhishek, havan and festivals
  • Clear label: for religious and ritual use only, not for drinking

Identity & creative direction

  • Logo and identity exploration
  • Deep navy, ivory and antique-gold palette
  • Label and packaging concepts, consistent typography

Digital experience

  • Website concept: story, origin, FAQs, enquiries
  • Android app prototype with product information and contact
  • Described as concepts until production readiness is verified

Marketing & storytelling

  • Coming Soon announcement concepts
  • Brand-introduction animations and short video concepts
  • Posters and social creatives

Business foundations

  • Proprietorship and GST registration preparation
  • Packaging and bottle-sourcing research
  • E-commerce planning and preliminary pricing

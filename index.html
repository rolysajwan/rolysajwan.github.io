01. Vision & positioning

Build a recognizable spiritual heritage brand rooted in the cultural and religious significance of Devprayag. The brand direction is guided by three principles:

  • Heritage: respect for the spiritual significance of the Ganga and Devprayag.
  • Presentation: elegant, considered visual identity and packaging.
  • Accessibility: helping customers discover the brand and understand its intended use through digital touchpoints.

02. Product concept

The initial concept is Devprayag Ganga Jal in a 250 ml bottle, intended exclusively for religious and ritual use.

  • Daily home worship and puja
  • Abhishek and other religious rituals
  • Havan, traditional ceremonies, festivals and special occasions
  • Clear product communication: “For religious and ritual use only. Not for drinking.”

03. Brand identity & creative direction

The creative direction combines premium Indian heritage, understated luxury and spiritual authenticity. The aim is a recognizable identity without excessive decoration or generic religious imagery.

  • Logo and identity exploration
  • Deep navy, ivory and antique-gold palette exploration
  • Bottle-label and outer-packaging concepts
  • Consistent typography, social assets and digital presentation
  • Visual storytelling inspired by Devprayag and the Himalayan region

04. Digital experience

Digital touchpoints are being planned to help people understand the brand, its origin and product purpose.

  • Website concept: brand story, Devprayag origin, product presentation, FAQs, religious-use information and enquiries.
  • Android app prototype: brand-focused home screen, product information, contact functionality and early navigation/interface concepts.

Website and app items should be described as concepts or prototypes until their production readiness is verified.

05. Marketing & storytelling

The goal is a consistent premium presence, with content that creates recognition and curiosity while staying respectful to the product's cultural context.

  • Coming Soon announcement concepts
  • Cinematic brand-introduction animations
  • Short-form promotional video concepts
  • Product-led advertising, posters and social creatives
  • Devprayag-inspired brand storytelling
  • Consistent logo and watermark use

06. Business foundations

The project also involves practical preparation behind the brand, connecting creative work with the steps needed to build a real small business.

  • Proprietorship and GST registration preparation
  • Business and premises-related documentation
  • Packaging and bottle-sourcing research
  • E-commerce marketplace planning
  • Preliminary pricing and product presentation
  • Customer communication and brand positioning
